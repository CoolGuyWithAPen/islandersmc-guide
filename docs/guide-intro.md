# Getting started

In this server, there are multiple commands you'll wanna remember, and in thid guide, you'll learn all about them!

When you first join the SMP, you'll be greeted with multiple NPCs around the spawn island. Feel free to explore and see what they do. The most important NPC you'll wanna know is the RTP, once you interact with him, he will bring you to a random spot in the world for you to explore and build your base at!
:::caution
Agility level 3 is required to teleport to your spawn point, so if you go back to spawn before reaching agility level 3, you **will** lose your base
:::
