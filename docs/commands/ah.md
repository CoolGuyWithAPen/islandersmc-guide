# Auction House

The auction house is a great spot to sell your goods for more cash, and buy items ou don't wanna grind for!

---

List of commands:
1. ```/ah```
    - Shows the main menu of the auction house.
2. ```/ah sell [price]```
    - Sells the item you are currently holding in your main hand for the amount you've set in the [price] argument.
3. ```/ah search [item]```
    - Searches the auction house for a desired item.
4. ```/ah collection-box```
    - Allows you to recieve the items you've just bought or take your expired lsitings.
5. ```/ah view [player]```
    - Shows what a specific player is selling.
6. ```/ah watch```
    - Creates a watch and will let you know when the item you want is up for sale.

:::note
```/ah watch``` is a paid command, it requires [I forget what rank] to use.
:::
