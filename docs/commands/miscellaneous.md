# Miscellaneous

Here you will find random commands that are still very helpful

---

1. ```/spawn```
    - Teleports you to spawn island.
2. ```/home```
    - Teleports you to your spawn point. 
        > **Requires agility level 3**
3. ```/shop```
    - Opens a neat shop menu to buy essentials for your island.
4. ```/menu```
    - Opens a menu with all sorts of quick access commands you might want.
5. ```/craft```
    - Opens up a crafting table.
