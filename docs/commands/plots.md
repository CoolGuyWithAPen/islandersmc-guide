# Land Claiming

Land claiming can be complicated, but I will make sure to make it as simple as possible!

### Claiming

Use ```/claim``` in every chunk you want to claim, make sure those chunks are next to each other. As you claim chunks, you should notice a green border appear, that shows the chunks you've claimed.

### Unclaiming

```/unclaim``` is the easiest way to unclaim the chunk you're currently standing in, but if you aren't at your claimed territory, use ```/region```, click on the region you want to unclaim chunks in, then go to chunk list and click the chunks you want to unclaim there.

### Managing your territory

```/region``` is a nice GUI to manage your plots, but if you'd rather commands, here is a list of some you should remember.

- ```/region borders```
    - shows your borders, and to stop, just use ```/region borders stop``` to hide them again.
- ```/region ban [username]```
    - bans a player from your territory, protecting it ever so much more.
- ```/region unban [username]```
    - unbans a player you've previously banned.
- ```/region banlist```
    - shows a list of players you've banned.
- ```/region trust [username]```
    - sends a request to the username you inputed to be a member in your territory.
- ```/region untrust [username]```
    - removes a member from your territory.

There are plenty of other commands, but these are the most important ones you'll wanna remember
