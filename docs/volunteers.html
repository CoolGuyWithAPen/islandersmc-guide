Volunteers

Islanders MC has many different volunteer ranks

Moderators​

In any case that moderation is required, feel free to ping any of these roles in the Discord, or ask for help from one of these guys.

  1. Owner
    • The owner role is given to CoolGuyWithAPen as he is indeed the owner of Islanders MC.
  2. Manager
    • Helps out with everything and can definetely help you out.
  3. Moderators
    • They are volunteers that have access to help in any conflict and keep chat tame.

Beta Testers​

Beta testers are given new features early and get to help make the server balanced! They come in 2 tiers, Beta Testers, and Temporary Beta Testers. Temporary Beta Testers are only used for huge testing purposes and only get to test one specific feature. Meanwhile Beta Testers are permenant, they test every new feature, and get rewards for doing so!

Support​

Volunteers with this rank are trained to answer questions, point you to the right direction, help with the store page and fix anything wrong that has happened.

Builder​

Builders are volunteers who help build the servers spawn, event island, parkour and more! They build in sandbox worlds where they can make stunning creations and get it added to Islanders MC for Spawn Island touches, a new lobby, an event island and more!

Applications​

You can apply to volunteer on Islanders MC on our Discord Server in the #information Channel! From there, you will select the role you want, input your username and write a short paragraph on why you think you deserve to be a part of the team. If you get accepted, you will undergo an interview. If that goes well, you're on the team! As easy as that.

A big thanks to all volunteers!
